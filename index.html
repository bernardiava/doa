✦ Questions ✦

You May Be Wondering...

What is inner growth analysis and why is it in a prayer app?
Inner growth analysis is the process of uncovering the hidden, suppressed parts of ourselves. Rather than generic prayers, DoaJiwa first listens for what your deeper self is asking beneath the surface, then translates that deeper truth into prayer. The result feels uncannily personal — because it is.
What is stored in my profile?
Every prayer you receive is saved with its date, religion, language, title, full text, and the personal insight. You can read them anytime, copy them, or delete individual prayers. Your raw thoughts input is also encrypted and stored privately, used only to improve personalisation over time — never shared.
I'm not religious. Can I still use this?
Absolutely. Choose "Spiritual / Universal / No Label." The prayer will be offered to the universe, the mystery of life, or your highest self — however you wish to address it.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, with no penalty. Monthly subscribers may cancel anytime and retain access until month end. Your prayer history is always preserved, even if you return to the free tier.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

At DoaJiwa, we believe your spiritual journey is deeply personal. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we store your name, email address, and subscription status.
  • Prayer Content: Your personal reflections and the prayers generated are stored privately in your account.
  • Usage Data: We track basic usage patterns (e.g., number of prayers created) to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalised prayers based on your faith tradition and language
  • To provide you with access to your prayer history and personal insights
  • To manage your subscription and billing through our payment processor (Xendit)
  • To improve our AI models and service quality

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is encrypted and stored securely. Your raw input thoughts are used only for generating your prayers and improving personalisation over time. We never share your personal content with third parties except as required by law or with your explicit consent.

4. Your Rights

  • Access all your stored data at any time via your profile page
  • Delete individual prayers or your entire prayer history
  • Delete your complete account and all associated data
  • Export your data upon request

5. Third-Party Services

We use Xendit for payment processing. Their privacy policy applies to payment transactions. We may use analytics tools to understand usage patterns, but these do not identify you personally.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@doajiwa.com
